Critical equipment precautions
  • Never plug or unplug the tow cable while the TCU is powered on.
  • Turn off TCU power before disconnecting the towfish; otherwise the towfish or TCU may be damaged.
  • Keep connectors clean and dry, and replace protective covers after use.
  • Follow current agency and vessel procedures for towing, deployment, and recovery.
